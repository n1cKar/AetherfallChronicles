Aetherfall Chronicles

An original endless low-poly isometric fantasy Action RPG built for the browser.

Engine: Three.js · TypeScript · Vite

Systems

  • Infinite procedural world generation
  • Action combat with combos, dodge, and ultimates
  • Procedural loot with 8 rarity tiers
  • Five playable classes with unique skill kits
  • Dynamic weather, day/night, and nine biomes
  • Multiplayer-ready architecture
